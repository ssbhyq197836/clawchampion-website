How ClawChampion works

ClawChampion lets you operate physical coin pushers and claw machines from your phone. You see the machine through live room video and use the controls shown in the app.

01

Choose a room

Open a machine room in the app. Check its live view, instructions, availability, and current GP cost before starting.

02

Control the machine

Use the on-screen controls to send an action to the physical machine. Watch the live view for its response; a remote connection can have delay.

03

Check the result

Follow the machine and the in-app record. For a missing response or an account discrepancy, use the room's fault/help option or contact support.

Real hardware, shown on video

The machine is physical. The app provides a live video view and remote controls; interface elements and progress indicators are digital. This is different from a game where the entire coin pusher is computer-generated.

See how a real remote machine differs from a virtual simulator. The current app and room instructions, rather than this page, govern costs and gameplay details.

If the room does not respond

Stop trying the control repeatedly. Note the room or machine name, your in-app UID, the time and time zone, what you pressed, and any GP you believe was affected. A screenshot or screen recording can help support find the specific game record.

Use the in-app help option or email customerservice@clawchampion.com. A report needs checking; this page does not promise that a result or compensation has been confirmed.
